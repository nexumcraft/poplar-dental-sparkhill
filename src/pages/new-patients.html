Register as a new patient

We're accepting new NHS patients for routine dental care. Here's who can join, how registering works, and what happens at your first visit.

Who we're accepting

  • Adults aged 18 and over
  • Adults who are entitled to free NHS dental care
  • Children and young people under 18

This is what the practice last confirmed on NHS.uk, in September 2026. Places can change, so we'll confirm yours when you get in touch.

How registering works

  1. Send your details, or call

    Use the short form below, or call 0121 771 3720 during opening hours. We only need your name and a phone number.

  2. We check we can take you

    We confirm we have an NHS place for you. If we can't take you right now, we'll tell you, and you can find another NHS dentist on NHS.uk.

  3. We book your first check-up

    We call you back to arrange your first appointment.

What to bring to your first visit

  • A list of any medicines you take
  • Proof if you get free NHS dental care, such as a maternity exemption certificate, an HC2 certificate or a benefit letter
  • Your previous dentist's details, if you have them
  • For children, a parent or guardian should come along

Your first check-up

We'll go through your medical history, examine your teeth, gums and mouth, and take X-rays if they're needed. If you need any treatment, we'll explain your options and the NHS charge before we start. For Band 2 or Band 3 treatment, you'll get a written treatment plan.
