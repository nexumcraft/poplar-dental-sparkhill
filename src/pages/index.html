New patients

New to Poplar?

We're accepting NHS patients for routine dental care:

  • Adults aged 18 and over
  • Adults who get free NHS dental care
  • Children and young people under 18

This is what the practice last confirmed on NHS.uk, in September 2026. Places can change, so we'll confirm yours when you get in touch.

{{> brush-scene}}

  1. Get in touch

    Call 0121 771 3720, or send your name and phone number using our short online form.

  2. We confirm your place

    We check we can take you as an NHS patient, then call you back to book your first check-up.

  3. Your first check-up

    We go through your medical history, check your teeth and gums, and explain any treatment and its NHS charge before we start.
