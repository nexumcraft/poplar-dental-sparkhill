Treatments

Everyday NHS dental care

The care most families need, from routine check-ups to root canal treatment. Each page explains what's involved and the NHS charge. How NHS charges work

Routine and preventive care

  • Check-ups and cleaningExaminations, X-rays when needed, and a scale and polish.
  • Gum disease treatmentHelp for bleeding or sore gums.
  • Braces and orthodonticsNHS braces for under-18s who qualify.

Restorative treatment

  • FillingsRepair decay before it gets worse.
  • Crowns and bridgesStrengthen a tooth or replace a missing one.
  • Root canal treatmentTreat infection so you can keep the tooth.
  • Tooth extractionsCareful removal, with clear aftercare.
Urgent dental careToothache, swelling or a broken tooth? Call us from 9am, or NHS 111 when we're closed.

New patients

New to Poplar?

We're accepting NHS patients for routine dental care:

  • Adults aged 18 and over
  • Adults who get free NHS dental care
  • Children and young people under 18

This is what the practice last confirmed on NHS.uk, in September 2026. Places can change, so we'll confirm yours when you get in touch.

  1. Get in touch

    Call 0121 771 3720, or send your name and phone number using our short online form.

  2. We confirm your place

    We check we can take you as an NHS patient, then call you back to book your first check-up.

  3. Your first check-up

    We go through your medical history, check your teeth and gums, and explain any treatment and its NHS charge before we start.
