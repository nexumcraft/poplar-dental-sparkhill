Fillings

A filling repairs a tooth damaged by decay or a small break, and stops the problem getting worse.

When you might need a filling

  • A tooth is sensitive to hot, cold or sweet food and drink
  • You can see or feel a hole, a dark spot or a rough edge
  • A small piece of tooth has broken off

Often, decay is found at a check-up before you feel anything at all, which is when it's quickest and simplest to fix.

What happens

  1. We numb the area with a local anaesthetic so you're comfortable.
  2. The dentist removes the decay and cleans the tooth.
  3. The tooth is filled, shaped and polished, and we check your bite feels right.

Most fillings are done in a single visit. Your dentist will recommend the filling material that's clinically right for your tooth and talk you through the options.

Afterwards

The numbness usually wears off within a few hours, so take care not to bite your cheek or lip in the meantime. A little sensitivity for a few days is normal. If the pain lasts, or your bite feels high, give us a call.
