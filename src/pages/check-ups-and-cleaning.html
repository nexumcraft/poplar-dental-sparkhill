Check-ups and cleaning

A regular check-up is the simplest way to keep your teeth and gums healthy, and to catch small problems before they become painful.

What happens at a check-up

  • We'll ask about your general health, any medicines you take, and anything you've noticed in your mouth.
  • The dentist examines your teeth, gums and mouth, including checking for early signs of mouth cancer.
  • We may take X-rays if the dentist needs a closer look between or beneath your teeth.
  • We'll explain anything we find and agree a plan with you before any treatment starts.

Scale and polish

If you need it, a scale and polish removes the hardened plaque (tartar) that brushing alone can't shift. On the NHS it's part of a Band 1 check-up when it's clinically needed.

How often should you come?

It depends on how healthy your teeth and gums are. Your dentist will recommend the right gap for you, which can be anywhere from 3 months to 2 years for adults, and up to 12 months for children.

Bringing your children

Bring your child to the dentist as soon as their first milk teeth appear. NHS dental care is free for under-18s, and early visits help children feel relaxed in the dental chair.
