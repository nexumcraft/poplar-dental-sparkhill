Crowns and bridges

A crown protects and strengthens a damaged tooth. A bridge fills the gap left by a missing tooth.

Crowns

A crown is a cap that covers the whole visible part of a tooth. Your dentist may recommend one if a tooth is badly decayed, broken or heavily filled, or to protect a tooth after root canal treatment.

Bridges

A bridge is one or more false teeth held in place by being fixed to the teeth on either side of the gap. It stays in your mouth, so you look after it much like your natural teeth.

What to expect

  1. At your first visit, the dentist prepares the tooth, takes a mould, and fits a temporary crown if you need one.
  2. Your crown or bridge is made to fit you by a dental laboratory.
  3. At your second visit, we fit it in place and check your bite.

Looking after them

Brush twice a day with a fluoride toothpaste and clean between your teeth every day. Under a bridge, floss threaders or small interdental brushes help. Regular check-ups let us make sure everything stays healthy.
