NHS charges

NHS dental charge bands and what each covers
BandWhat it covers
Band 1Examination, diagnosis and advice. Includes X-rays, a scale and polish, and fluoride varnish if clinically needed.
Band 2Everything in Band 1, plus fillings, root canal treatment, tooth extractions and more extensive gum treatment.
Band 3Everything in Bands 1 and 2, plus crowns, bridges, dentures and orthodontic treatment such as braces.
UrgentUrgent care to relieve pain or stop a problem getting worse. It costs the same as Band 1.

You pay one charge for each course of treatment, even if it takes more than one visit. If you need treatment from different bands, you only pay the highest one. Charges are set nationally: see current NHS dental charges on NHS.uk.

Who gets free NHS dental care

In England, NHS dental treatment is free if, when treatment starts, you are:

  • Under 18, or under 19 and in full-time education
  • Pregnant, or have had a baby in the last 12 months
  • Getting certain benefits, such as income-related Employment and Support Allowance, Pension Credit Guarantee Credit, or Universal Credit below a set income, or you're the partner or dependant of someone who does
  • Named on a valid NHS tax credit exemption certificate or HC2 certificate

Please bring proof to your appointment. If you claim free treatment you aren't entitled to, you may have to pay a penalty. Check who can get free NHS dental treatment on NHS.uk.

Access

If you use a wheelchair, have limited mobility, or need any other help to visit us, call us before your appointment so we can make sure we're ready for you. There's a bus stop close to the practice.

Feedback and complaints

We want to hear about your experience, good or bad. Most concerns can be sorted out quickly by talking to us.

  1. Talk to us. Ask for the practice manager, call us on 0121 771 3720, or write to us at the practice. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 3 working days and explain what happens next.
  2. Or contact the NHS commissioner instead. If you'd rather not complain to us directly, you can complain to NHS Birmingham and Solihull Integrated Care Board, which commissions dental services locally. You can complain to us or to them, but not both.
  3. If you're still unhappy. You can ask the Parliamentary and Health Service Ombudsman to look into it. Call 0345 015 4033 or visit ombudsman.org.uk.

Complaints should normally be made within 12 months. You can get free, independent help from an NHS complaints advocate, or from Healthwatch Birmingham and Solihull.

Privacy notice

Poplar Dental Practice is responsible for the personal information you give us.

What we collect through this website

If you use our registration form, we collect your name, phone number, whether you're registering an adult or a child, and anything else you choose to tell us, such as your email address or the best time to call. Please don't include medical information in the form. We use this only to contact you about registering and to set up your patient record. We don't use it for marketing, and we never sell it.

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The registration form sends your details to our automation service (n8n), which saves them to a secure Google Sheet used by the practice team. Google is based in the United States and processes this information with safeguards for transfers from the UK.

How long we keep it

If you become a patient, your details become part of your dental record, which we keep for the period NHS rules require. We delete form submissions from our website host once we've contacted you, and we delete the details of anyone who doesn't go on to register once we no longer need them.

The chat assistant

The "Ask a question" chat on this website is an automated assistant. It answers using the information on this website and can book appointments, but it can't see patient records. Your messages are processed by our automation service (n8n) and by Google's Gemini AI service to write a reply. They're kept for a short time so the conversation makes sense and so we can check the assistant is working properly. If you book an appointment, the assistant asks for your name, phone number, whether the patient is an adult or child, whether you're new or existing, a short reason for the visit and, if you like, your email address. These details are saved to our appointment list and diary (Google Sheets and Google Calendar), used only to manage your appointment, and deleted when no longer needed. Please don't share medical information in the chat.

Cookies and third parties

This website doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. The map is provided by OpenStreetMap, and our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Both may see your IP address when the page loads.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Call us or write to us at the practice. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office.
