NHS dental charge bands and what each covers
BandWhat it covers
Band 1Examination, diagnosis and advice. Includes X-rays, a scale and polish, and fluoride varnish if clinically needed.
Band 2Everything in Band 1, plus fillings, root canal treatment, tooth extractions and more extensive gum treatment.
Band 3Everything in Bands 1 and 2, plus crowns, bridges, dentures and orthodontic treatment such as braces.
UrgentUrgent care to relieve pain or stop a problem getting worse. It costs the same as Band 1.

You pay one charge for each course of treatment, even if it takes more than one visit. If you need treatment from different bands, you only pay the highest one. Charges are set nationally: see current NHS dental charges on NHS.uk.
