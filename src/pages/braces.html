Braces and orthodontics

Straighter teeth can be easier to keep clean and more comfortable to bite with. We'll check whether braces are right for you or your child, and explain the options.

NHS braces for children

On the NHS, braces are available for under-18s who have a clear clinical need, for example teeth that are badly crowded or a bite that causes problems. Dentists use a standard scale, the Index of Orthodontic Treatment Need, to decide who qualifies. Treatment that's mainly for appearance isn't usually available on the NHS.

What happens

  1. We check your child's teeth and bite, usually as part of a routine check-up.
  2. If braces could help, we explain what's involved and arrange treatment or a referral to an orthodontic specialist.
  3. There may be a waiting list for NHS orthodontic treatment, so it helps to ask early.

Braces for adults

NHS braces are rarely available for adults, who usually need to go private. Ask us at your next check-up and we'll explain what might work for you.

NHS Band 3
