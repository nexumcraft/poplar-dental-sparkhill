Gum disease treatment

Gum disease is very common and often painless at first. Treated early, it can be stopped before it damages the bone that holds your teeth in place.

Signs to look out for

  • Gums that bleed when you brush or floss
  • Red, swollen or sore gums
  • Bad breath or a bad taste that won't go away
  • Gums pulling back from your teeth
  • Teeth that feel loose

How we treat it

  • We show you the best way to brush and clean between your teeth at home.
  • A scale and polish removes the plaque and tartar that cause inflammation.
  • If the disease is more advanced, we clean more deeply below the gum line, using a local anaesthetic if needed.
  • We check how your gums are healing at a follow-up visit, and refer you to a specialist if needed.

Lowering your risk

Brush twice a day with a fluoride toothpaste, clean between your teeth every day, and keep up with your check-ups. Smoking and poorly controlled diabetes both raise the risk of gum disease, so stopping smoking and managing your blood sugar make a real difference.
