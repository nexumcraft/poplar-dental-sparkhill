Our standards

Inspected and registered

We're registered with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), which inspects dental practices in England. At our last inspection, in August 2022, inspectors found the practice visibly clean and well maintained, with infection control that followed national guidance, and staff who knew how to deal with medical emergencies.

Read our CQC report

Registered professionals

Every dentist and dental nurse working in the UK must be registered with the General Dental Council (GDC). You can check anyone's registration on the GDC website.
